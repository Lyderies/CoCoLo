## Family decisions

The complete SQL-reader inventory and retention/size decisions are in
[s14b/readers.md](s14b/readers.md). Existing OP-2 already bounds finished
account_open_receipts, account_open_requests and project-owned host_run_bindings;
its released binding hold is the durable open-expired answer. Keep that policy.

Keep the small session/provenance/launch/capacity/hold rows for the project lifetime:
they prevent replay, anchor completion and recovery, and preserve ingestion counts.
Keep outcomes needed by live or controlled evidence for learning and historical review; unreferenced
outcomes follow the existing 90-day learning window and leave status summaries.
Size all permanent metadata as unswept in S14b-1. This is a five-year envelope,
not a claim that permanent evidence has a finite steady-state size. Owner-configured
maximum event retention already exceeds this envelope and is outside this step.

Provider/admission/dispatch detail becomes a closed `retention_summary` record;
it records exact counts by original type and source and the original observation
range. It does not claim to reconstruct removed detail. Retain summary vocabulary
and all old validators. Active admission evidence stays in original form, because
its content and signatures remain decision authority.

Tests precede implementation. Evidence, platform limits and primary reruns go in
[s14b/STATUS.md](s14b/STATUS.md). No assertion present on the base is removed or weakened.

Reader-driven revision before outcome implementation: `run_learning_cycle` reads
outcomes within `LEARNING_RETENTION_MS`, while controlled evidence has explicit
links and attestations. The measured released-run test disproved the initial
keep-every-outcome sizing assumption. Its replacement assertions are mapped in
`s14b/assertions.md`; a new unreferenced-outcome test must fail before that code changes.

## Fix-round interpretation (040-s14b-r1)

A derivation edge between distinct expired, sweepable members of the same run
is internal history, not a live dependency. Sweep its complete bounded group
atomically, including terminal host events and the summary. An edge to anything
outside that group, explicit control, a live binding/open/slot or a replay fence
still preserves the whole group. Oversized groups remain full conservatively.

Historical answers require indexed membership proof and a matching type when
requested. Unknown and mistyped IDs remain not found. Read-budget refusal cannot
be relabelled as evidence of a past record. Retained summaries are the durable
source for a rebuildable connection-local membership index; backups need no new
persistent table or schema version.
