### Measurement protocol

Decisions rest on counts and work (releases per batch, batches, rows selected per batch, SQLite VM steps, rows backed
off); wall-clock is a loose bound. Scenarios run on fresh stores three times and each batch position's figure is the
median over the repeats; B1 is one warm-up run then the median of five. A single 30 ms pause on a loaded machine
therefore moves one sample, not the median. B1's commit cost is measured as a paired difference (same transaction
without and with the register's writes, interleaved, nine pairs), because the register shares existing transactions.
The production-path figure uses the driver's real `_expire_results` and the real writer lane on a virtual clock (the
10 s cadence is counted, not slept; each pass's real duration is added).

Already covered elsewhere (confirmed, not re-written): RS-10 lock under 750 ms, 2 concurrent starts, queue 1,024,
start-up +200 ms with 1,000 due rows and the starter's 60 s drain are E2c-3 (`docs/requirements/e2c/requirements.md`);
B2 (+100 ms start-up), B3 (batch of 64 under 50 ms), B4 (equal work at 0/100,000 tombstones) and B5 are
`tests.test_b7b_budgets` (RS-B-B7-5); RS-D S3's six budget tests are `root:test_rsd_budget`
(RS-D-9-S2/S3, `docs/requirements/rsd-s3/`), rerun here and passing (see STATUS).

## Scope and limits of what is measured

- B1 times every outermost call into the register (`take_hold`, `arm_hold`, `release_hold`, `wake_before`,
  `wake_settled`) and the whole of `project_release_ack._release_obligation` (its reservation/hold JOIN, the release),
  made while a real lease-backed run is reserved, armed, bound, published and its obligation released. It adds the commit
  cost the register's rows add (paired difference) and the memory-hold writer transactions (`measure_b7b.memory_writer_samples`).
  The account receipt verification before that release is not part of the register's work.
- B6 observable budget and the section 5 mechanism are separate rows. The product's lease start pass
  `release_earlier_unarmed_leases` fetches every reserved lease on every batch and keeps no back-off, so the mechanism row
  is a finding. The unattended orchestration close (batch of 4, back-off 10 s to 5 min) is the C6 row, covered by
  `tests.test_b6_unattended_close`, not by this budget.
- `tests.test_b7b_budgets` still labels B1 and B6 `unmeasured` for its own narrower scope; this unit supersedes that label
  and leaves that test untouched.
